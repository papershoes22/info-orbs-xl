# The original case base

`bottom_20mmscrews.stl` is the base of **"Info Orbs enclosure" by Skeetz1230**, published on Printables
(https://www.printables.com/model/1016115-info-orbs-enclosure, 2024-11-07) under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International** licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is included unchanged so `make_base.py`
can rebuild the deeper base from it.

`../base_xl_3mm.stl`, `../print_base_xl_3mm.stl` and `../orbs_base_xl_petg.3mf` are a remix of it, under the same
CC BY 4.0 licence. Changes: the part below z -23.5 mm moved down 3 mm (the inside and the screw-head holes 3 mm
deeper), and the inner faces of the two middle posts cut back so a USB-C plug 10.97 mm wide passes between them.
Made with `../make_base.py`.
